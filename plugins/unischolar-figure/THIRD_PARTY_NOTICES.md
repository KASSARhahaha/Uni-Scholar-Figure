# Third-party notices

## FigEdit

The plugin includes a reduced source snapshot of [FigEdit](https://github.com/giszzt/figedit) at commit `acc4f5c01506fe10dad99ca755a9612d67de5467`. The snapshot keeps its skill instructions, scripts, references, templates, and dependency metadata, while omitting large example images. This plugin adds the missing `python-pptx` runtime declaration to the copied requirements file. FigEdit is licensed under the MIT License. Its notice and license are preserved under `licenses/`.

FigEdit's SVG-to-PPTX layer includes work adapted from [PPT Master](https://github.com/hugohe3/ppt-master), also under the MIT License. The upstream notice and license are preserved under `licenses/` and in the vendored source tree.
